Our hotel

A little different. A lot more personal.

An old brick house that likes having guests.

We're a small hotel in the historic heart of Tbilisi. Inside you'll find classic furniture, floral fabrics, gold frames and lamps that each have their own idea of light.

We kept the thick walls, the carved door and the wrought iron, then added a light new roof with glass domes and a terrace. It feels less like checking in and more like visiting someone with good taste and a garden.
