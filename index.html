The magnolia in our courtyard

Our magnolia is 127 years old. We're the new ones here.

Long before there was a hotel, there was this tree. It grows in our courtyard, between old brick and new glass, and reaches past the top floor.

Take your coffee down and sit under it for a while. It has seen 127 Tbilisi summers. It's in no hurry, and neither should you be.
